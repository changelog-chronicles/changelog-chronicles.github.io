dlt 1.0.0 breaking

2024-09-16 · release notes

Breaking: Major release: foreign_key hint is dropped in favor of nested references, the complex data type is deprecated for json, and terminal job errors now abort the load package; review the linked breaking changes list.

Use the rest_api, sql_database and filesystem sources directly from dlt core, plus a new SQLAlchemy destination and SFTP support for the filesystem source.
